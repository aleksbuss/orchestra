> **⏸ EVALUATED & DEFERRED (2026-08-07, PM #98) — replacing the time-bound CI scan with a type-level chokepoint that injects deadlines automatically.** Proposed by a model council; verdict after a second council round (3/3 agreed, matching my own): **do it as a SEPARATE PR later, or not at all — never bundled with a bug fix.** Do not re-litigate without new information.
>
> Why not now: (1) **"the compiler replaces the grep" is false** — TypeScript cannot forbid an `import { generateText } from "ai"`, so a chokepoint MOVES the CI scan from "inspect the options object" to "inspect the imports"; the gate does not disappear. (2) **The premise does not hold here, measured:** all 15 callsites pass `callDeadlineSignal(signal)` and the budget comes from the default — `callDeadlineSignal(undefined)` still returns a BOUNDED signal, a negative budget falls back to the default, and only an explicit `0` (the operator env hatch) removes the bound. A callsite cannot accidentally unbound a call ([`stream-watchdog.test.ts`](src/lib/agent/stream-watchdog.test.ts), "fail-safe by construction"). (3) **Budgets are deliberately non-uniform** — streaming uses the watchdog and MUST NOT get a total-duration cap; proposers and judges have their own. A single injecting wrapper needs a per-call policy argument, reintroducing the decision it was meant to abolish. (4) Type-inference risk: `generateObject`/`streamText` are generic; a sloppy wrapper invites `any`.
>
> **Conceded, and the reason to revisit:** the scan's real hole is not a typo, it is **SDK migration** — it greps for `generateText`/`generateObject`/`streamText`, so a move to a different SDK makes new unbounded calls invisible to it. Also conceded: framing (1) as "the gate does not disappear" is unfairly literal; the chokepoint's actual value is that the SAFE path becomes the default and the unsafe one becomes deliberate. **Trigger to revisit: any decision to change or add an LLM SDK.** If built, the shape is a wrapper taking an explicit policy (`stream-watchdog | call-deadline | custom`) plus an import gate in the style of [`agent-preflight-gate.test.ts`](src/lib/agent/agent-preflight-gate.test.ts).
